# Instagram Trial Reels — controlled experiment playbook
Updated 22 October 2026. Source: creator video transcript supplied by Rocío (Valeria, personal branding photographer; creator claim of 3,000 followers overnight is unverified).

## Purpose and limits
Test *discovery* hooks with non-followers before choosing a version for Ocean Calling's regular feed. Treat this as a hypothesis, not a guaranteed reach or follower-growth strategy. Instagram eligibility, feature UI and distribution behaviour can change; confirm in the publishing account. Trial Reels already exist in the Notion Content Experiment Engine as one allowable test method; do not create a separate tracking system.

## Workflow
1. Choose one Ocean Calling footage set whose creator/permissions are verified and one factual, approved story. Start with an editable master in Instagram Edits or the team's preferred editor.
2. Define ONE experimental variable (e.g. first-frame/on-screen hook) and one primary metric. Keep duration, footage sequence, music, posting window, caption, subtitles and CTA as constant as practical.
3. Produce control + one or two variants per batch. Four hook ideas may be brainstormed, but the existing engine allows at most control + 2 concurrently. Use Trial Reels where available to reach non-followers without first posting to the main feed.
4. At 24 hours log an *early* read, not a winner verdict: initial retention/skip, average watch time if available, shares, follows, and any graph dip. Repeat at 48 hours and 7 days for more reliable comparison. Track time posted, audience/exposure differences, sample sizes, impressions and other confounders.
5. Diagnose an early drop as one possible clue about the hook; it can also come from audience mismatch, delivery, visual or technical issues. A later dip identifies a segment worth reviewing, not proof of the cause.
6. Only after reviewing data create an original final version for the regular feed; use the best-supported combination, but do not claim small creative tweaks defeat Instagram duplicate-content rules. Never post automatically. Rocío approves before publishing.
7. Write results to the EXISTING Notion Growth experiments & learnings, Content Intelligence and Reels Insights records. Promote a general rule only when repeated tests satisfy the engine's existing evidence/approval requirements.

## Four working first-frame hooks for one ethical whale-encounter Reel
These are alternatives for testing the same licensed/approved footage; confirm the story and visual match before use.
- Transformation: "The whale moment looks different when you stop trying to control it."
- Promise: "Before booking a whale encounter, check these 3 things."
- Negative: "Don't choose a whale trip based on this one video."
- Visual: "Look at the distance between the swimmers and the whale."
For a fair test, don't publish all four simultaneously by default; test one variable, choose comparable windows and keep a fixed control.
