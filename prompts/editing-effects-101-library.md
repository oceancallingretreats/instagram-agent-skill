## Ocean Calling usage rules

- Marine science: verify anatomy and behavior; use doodles, callouts, dotted motion paths; label AI recreations.
- Retreat desire and bookings: favor cinematic, natural travel imagery, collages from real guest/destination photos, and clear booking CTAs.
- Instagram editing: do not obscure eyes, mouths or features; respect safe zones, preserve brand assets and original app characters.
- E-commerce: use product background isolation, neutrals, lifestyle scenes, object removal and consistent shadows.
- Review every output for true physical details, safe cropping and mobile readability.
