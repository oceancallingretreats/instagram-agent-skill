# Attention Trap — Ethical Reel Storytelling

Saved 2026-11-11. Apply to future Ocean Calling Reels, carousels, retreat stories and educational storytelling.

1. **Stakes**: Establish a relatable protagonist, meaningful consequences and a genuine deadline only where one actually exists.
2. **Big question**: Give viewers one compelling curiosity question they want resolved.
3. **Head fake**: Present a truthful, evidence-supported surprise that changes expectations; never fabricate a twist.
4. **Rehook**: Resolve one question before opening the next relevant question, with a satisfying final payoff.

**Production checklist**: A strong opening in the first 1–2 seconds; clear visual proof; concise captions; natural pacing and suspense; scientific claim verification and sources in the caption; no fake urgency, fabricated stakes or manipulative gambling claims.

**Example (sand tiger shark)**: Hook: 'Before they're born, sand tiger shark embryos may eat their siblings.' Question: 'How does this happen inside the womb?' Twist: 'Embryos can consume smaller siblings, and typically one survivor remains per uterus.' Rehook: 'Why would this evolutionarily unusual process happen?' Verify each detail against primary research before publishing.
