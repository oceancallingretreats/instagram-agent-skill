# Four reusable Instagram hook categories

## Hook categories from @katrinalebar — 11 November 2026

Source: four user-supplied Instagram carousel screenshots, slides 2–5 of 5, credited @katrinalebar / @socialbykatrina. Slide 1 was not supplied. Reference templates, not guaranteed performance.

### Controversial / Opinion
- Can we stop pretending that [common industry belief]?
- Not every [popular strategy] is worth your time.
- Just because everyone is doing [trend] doesn't mean you should.
- I don't think [common advice] is the answer, and here's why.
- Something I will NEVER recommend when it comes to [topic].
- Let's stop confusing [concept A] with [concept B].
- The problem isn't [obvious assumption]. It's something else entirely.
- This might be an uncomfortable conversation, but…
- You don't need more [common solution]. You need better [alternative].
- Can we retire the idea that [outdated belief]?

### Educational
- 3 things you need to know before you [action].
- The biggest mistake people make when trying to [desired result].
- Stop doing [common mistake] if you want to [result].
- Here's what I wish more people understood about [topic].
- 5 things making it harder for you to [result].
- Before you spend money on [product/service], watch this.
- The one thing you should ALWAYS do before [action].
- Here's why [common strategy] isn't giving you the results you want.
- What actually matters when it comes to [topic].
- 3 things I'd stop doing immediately if I wanted [result].

### Black Friday / commerce
- The Black Friday deal you didn't know you needed for [problem].
- Here's why you should grab [product/service] before [real deadline].
- Everything you need to know before buying [product/service] this Black Friday.
- If I were shopping for [category] this Black Friday, this is what I'd buy.
- Stop scrolling if [problem] is something you've wanted to fix.
- What makes [product/service] worth buying during this Black Friday sale?
- 3 reasons why [product/service] should be on your Black Friday wishlist.
- Here's what you get with [offer] before [real deadline].
- If you've been waiting for [product/service] to go on sale, here's your reminder.
- What I'd recommend to anyone looking to [result] this Black Friday.
- This is your chance to invest in [outcome] without paying [full price] (only if true).

### November / end of year
- POV: It's November and you're finally ready to take [goal] seriously.
- Things I'm doing differently this November to [result].
- If [goal] is still on your 2026 vision board, this is your reminder.
- Before the year ends, make sure you're doing these [number] things for [area].
- Consider this your sign to stop putting off [goal].
- Here's what I'd be focusing on if I wanted [result] before December.
- The November reset I didn't know I needed for [topic].
- Things to stop doing before entering 2027 if you want [goal].
- If I had the next 30 days to improve [area], here's where I'd start.
- A reminder that you still have time to [realistic result] this year.

### Application to Ocean Calling SOP
1. First ask what emotion the Reel should transmit; preserve source footage's emotional tone. Never make everything funny, spicy or controversial.
2. Check energy and production preference already known before proposing filming.
3. Choose 5 genuinely relevant hooks, recommend 1, and specify opening visual plus truthful payoff. Align with Illusion of Novelty and Triple Hook when appropriate.
4. Verify every wildlife/scientific claim, follow welfare and footage-credit rules; do not fabricate urgency, results, scarcity, endorsements or discounts.
5. Keep existing 2,100–2,200 character caption rule with science references, source credit and contextual CTA; measure first-second retention, watch time, completion, shares, saves and follows.
6. For independent e-commerce use, focus on the buyer problem, product evidence, real offer details, and accurate deadlines; do not restrict to ocean or retreat products.
7. Compare experiments instead of assuming these templates cause virality.

Example prompts for Ocean Calling (ideas, verify before publication): “Can we stop pretending touching marine wildlife is harmless?”; “3 things to know before swimming with sharks.”; “Before buying freediving equipment this Black Friday, watch this.”; “If learning to freedive is still on your 2026 vision board, here's your reminder.”
