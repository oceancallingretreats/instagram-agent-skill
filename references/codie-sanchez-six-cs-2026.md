# Codie Sanchez — Six C's Content Business Framework (2026)

Source: Codie Sanchez, “Don’t Become a Content Creator, Build a Content Business,” July 21, 2026.
https://codie-sanchez.convella.com/don-t-become-a-content-creator-build-a-content-business
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZvAwqGH_79Q

## Six C's
1. **Channel** — Prioritize one platform where audience, abilities and revenue opportunity align; expand only after developing traction.
2. **Credibility** — Earn trust through experience, demonstrated actions, and platform-native communication.
3. **Category** — Become clearly associated with one valuable and recognizable area of expertise.
4. **Curate** — Find content opportunities from real customer questions, market signals, proven formats and unanswered needs; do not copy blindly.
5. **Calibrate** — Review actual audience, retention, engagement and conversion results; improve through testing rather than volume alone.
6. **Collect** — Build owned channels (email/newsletter) and commercially useful offerings; distinguish followers from customers.

## Channel notes from original
- Instagram: relatable and aspirational stories, behind-the-scenes, shareable reels and carousels; commercial performance varies.
- YouTube: longer-lived searchable educational storytelling; builds depth of trust.
- TikTok: fast hooks to cold audiences; useful for awareness and easy-to-demonstrate products.
- LinkedIn: tactical professional education and practical lead magnets.
- X: sharp opinions, quick cycles; not necessarily best for sales.
- Newsletter: owned relationship, less algorithm dependence; useful for qualified conversion.

## Application to Ocean Calling and future brands (our recommendations, not Sanchez's claims)
- Prioritize Instagram as existing audience channel; test conversion before expansion.
- Pair playful, accurate wildlife education with story-driven trust and ethical ocean practices.
- Develop clear content pillars, not endless repetition of the same animal or hook.
- Collect opt-in emails for retreats, future products, and offers; avoid spam.
- Measure qualified enquiries, email signups and actual sales alongside shares, watch time and saves.
- Adapt these principles to other product niches rather than assuming one tactic works for everyone.
