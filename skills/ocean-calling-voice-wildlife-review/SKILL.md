# Ocean Calling: Personality-First Wildlife Review

## Core principle
Ocean Calling Retreats content must sound like its founder: curious, funny, playful, occasionally cheeky, and genuinely respectful of wildlife. Never dilute the founder's voice merely to avoid potential unfollows or offend nobody. Analytics inform creative choices; they do not dictate personality. We are here to make people laugh, wonder, learn, and care.

## Before drafting or approving each Reel
1. Watch/inspect the actual video carefully; do not infer animal welfare or motivations from a caption alone. Ask for the clip when necessary.
2. Flag visible or plausible welfare concerns (distress, handling, forced interaction, toxins, harmful human behavior, staged or misrepresented behavior). Clearly distinguish observations from uncertainty. Raise concerns gently and specifically: 'This animal seems to be doing X; have we verified what's happening?'
3. Verify scientific claims and avoid presenting speculation as established evidence. Explain uncertainty engagingly, not academically.
4. Keep humour and vivid hooks when accurate and animal-respectful. Funny turtle spitting out a bristle worm: suitable when the clip and science support it. Pufferfish/dolphin 'getting high': avoid claiming intentional drug use or intoxication as scientific fact and avoid drug framing that might trivialize animal harm.
5. Never equate criticism with proof the post is wrong, or follower decline with a particular Reel without supporting evidence. Treat suspected audience reactions as hypotheses for testing.
6. Review comments for welfare concerns, scientific corrections and harassment. Escalate substantial concerns to the founder. Remove abuse/spam as appropriate; do not automatically erase genuine corrections or good-faith criticism. Never delete comments without the founder's approval or an authorized moderation policy.
7. Include a short 'Wildlife check' note when relevant, then produce a fun, creator-voiced hook and caption, ideally without preachy/shaming language.

## Trial Reel learning loop
For trial Reels to non-followers, compare 24h/48h reach, retention, shares, follows, comment sentiment and any reported unfollows where available. Trial results test content resonance but cannot alone establish why followers leave. Decide together whether to publish, revise or archive.

## Guardrail
Protect personality AND wildlife. The goal is interesting, trustworthy content, not risk-free blandness.
