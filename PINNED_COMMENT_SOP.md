# Ocean Calling — Pinned Comment SOP (1 November 2026)

## Mandatory voice
Pinned comments on Instagram wildlife Reels should be short, cheeky, playful, specific to the actual animal/scene, and sound like Rocío. Make people smile while naturally inviting them to learn about Ocean Calling's ethical ocean retreats or expeditions. Vary the jokes and structure; avoid generic engagement questions, repetitive ad copy, stiff marketing, or shaming. The standard response CTA is **“Comment LINK”**, not “link in bio”. Match the offer to an actual available destination/expedition; do not imply retreats in fjords, guaranteed sightings, or interactions with wildlife. The Reel footage does not necessarily depict an experience Ocean Calling offers.

## Approved tone examples (user-selected)
**Turtle slap:** We can't promise the turtles will behave, but we can promise an ocean adventure 😂🐢 Discover our retreats — comment LINK ✨

**Manta ray:** No wings, no problem. These underwater angels know how to fly! 🖤 Want to discover our ocean adventures? Comment LINK and we'll show you where ✨

**Humpback whale:** We can't promise a humpback whale will sing you a love song, but seeing one in the wild is pretty magical! 😂🐋 Comment LINK to discover our ethical whale encounters ✨

**Orca (Mexico):** We can't promise an orca will bring you lunch, but we can take you on an orca adventure in Mexico! 😂🐋🇲🇽 Discover our ethical ocean expeditions — comment LINK for more info ✨

The playful “can't promise” line is one approach, **not** a compulsory formula. Prioritize a different original joke for each Reel. When using an offer-specific CTA, confirm the trip is actually offered and accurately described. Never promise sightings or solicit harassment of marine life.

## Production checklist
For every Reel: (1) identify exact footage and actual wildlife; (2) propose one or two distinct cheeky pinned comments, based on the scene; (3) align with currently available ethical retreats/expeditions; (4) include clear “Comment LINK” CTA; (5) verify wildlife welfare and no false trip/sighting implications. Keep caption CTAs and pinned-comment CTAs separate.
